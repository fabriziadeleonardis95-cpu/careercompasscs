Your organizational structure
Individual Contributor (IC)
One IC path in Customer Success: the Customer Success Manager owns a book of accounts end to end — growth, renewals and churn prevention. At each level — IC1 → IC2 → IC3 — you gain autonomy, take on more complex accounts and shape the team's playbook.
Manager track
Lead the Customer Success team. The manager track goes from Team Lead (M3) to Customer Success Manager (M4), Head of Customer Success (M5) and the VP of Customer Success, who also leads Onboarding and Care. Growth means coaching teams, scaling strategy across markets and shaping the playbook.

The foundations
Universal
Expected at every role at Amenitiz, regardless of track or seniority. The company's shared baseline — what makes someone effective here, no matter what they do.
Examples: Customer Centricity · Business Acumen · Operational Excellence
Your craft
Role-specific
The technical and functional skills unique to your discipline. As you grow within a track, you go deeper here — this is what defines your expertise.
Examples: Product Knowledge · CRM Architecture · Customer Empathy & Communication · Quality Focus
Leadership edge
Seniority-gated
Unlock as you grow into broader scope — typically from IC3 upward. About influence, mentorship and strategy. Often the difference between executing well and leading a function.
Examples: Influence & Persuasion · Relationship Management · Strategic Thinking · Decisiveness
